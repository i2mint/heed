heed.sinks.base

The sink abstraction — where a report goes (strategy pattern).

A sink is the only thing that decides what becomes of a report. The ingest layer calls Sink.submit(); richer sinks may also offer duplicate detection and acknowledgement. New destinations are added by writing a sink, never by editing a dispatcher.
